# SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION

## 1.1 Aadhaar Overview
Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identification number issued by the Unique Identification
Authority of India (UIDAI) to residents of India after they satisfy the verification process
described in Section 2.

## 1.2 Who can get Aadhaar?
Any individual in India, irrespective of age and gender, who is a resident of India or a
Non-Resident Indian (NRI), can enrol for an Aadhaar number. Only one Aadhaar number is
generated per individual (uniqueness enforced via demographic and biometric de-duplication).

**1.2.1 Aadhaar for residents of India** — Eligible if you have resided in India for 182 days or
more (in aggregate) in the 12 months immediately preceding the enrolment application.

**1.2.2 Aadhaar for NRIs** — NRIs can enrol with a valid Indian Passport (mandatory POI). The
182-day rule does not apply to NRIs.

## 1.3 Features of Aadhaar
- Aadhaar is an identifier: a 12-digit unique ID using demographic info (name, DOB, gender,
  address), face photo, fingerprints and iris. Mobile number and email are optional but recommended.
- Aadhaar is an ID, nothing more: it confers no right of citizenship or domicile, is a random
  number devoid of intelligence, and cannot be used to profile people by caste, religion, income,
  health, or geography.

## 1.4 Usage of Aadhaar
Provides a single-source offline/online identity verification usable repeatedly, eliminating the
need to re-submit identity documents for services, benefits and subsidies.

# SECTION 2: HOW TO ENROL FOR AADHAAR?

## 2.1 Enrolment Process
Steps: Visit an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre → Fill the Enrolment/Update Form → Give demographic
and biometric data → Submit valid POI, POA and DOB documents for scanning → Check &
confirm all data on-screen before the operator submits the packet → Collect your original
documents plus an acknowledgement slip containing the EID (Enrolment ID).

If enrolling under Head of Family (HoF) based enrolment, only a Proof of Relationship (PoR)
document is needed.

Exceptions exist for residents without documents in their own name (family members, homeless,
orphans) and for residents with biometric exceptions.

### 2.1.1 Document Based Enrolment
Provide a valid POI and valid POA per the UIDAI list.
- POI — Mandatory
- POA — Mandatory
- DOB — Submit a valid DOB document, or declare DOB/approximate age if unavailable.

If you lack documents in your name, a certificate issued by an authorized official (UIDAI standard
format, see Annexure II) can be used instead.

### 2.1.2 Head of Family (HoF) Based Enrolment
For family members without individual documents. Requires: Name of HoF, Proof of Relationship
(PoR) of resident and HoF, HoF's Aadhaar number, and biometric confirmation of the HoF at time
of enrolment.

### 2.1.3 Introducer Based Enrolment
Used if neither of the above is available. Introducers are verified officials appointed by
registrars: registrars' own employees, elected local body members, members of local
administrative bodies, postman, teachers, health workers, doctors, Anganwadi/Asha workers,
representatives of local NGOs. Captured info: Introducer's name, Introducer's Aadhaar number,
one biometric modality of the Introducer.

### 2.1.4 Enrolment of Children
**Below 5 years:** Requires Aadhaar authentication by a parent/guardian. Biometric data is NOT
captured for under-5s — only name, DOB, gender and photograph, linked to the parent/guardian's
Aadhaar. Documents needed: child's Birth Certificate (or hospital discharge card/slip for a
newborn) + Aadhaar/enrolment slip of one parent (preferably mother if both alive) or guardian. If
you need to update your own address, do so before enrolling the child so the child gets the
updated address.

**Age 5 to 18:** Documents needed: child's own School ID (from a recognised institution) or other
UIDAI-acceptable document; OR, if unavailable, HoF-based enrolment with a PoR document.
Parent/guardian gives consent on behalf of the minor.

### 2.1.5 Enrolment of Residents with Biometric Exceptions
For missing finger/eye etc., capture: complete demographic information, one iris (if both can't be
captured), fingerprints of remaining fingers, and an exception photograph.

## 2.2 Points to remember for Aadhaar Enrolment
- Carefully check all details in English AND regional language before the operator submits them.
- Aadhaar data is stored in English (default) plus a regional language of your choice: Hindi, Telugu,
  Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bangla, Odia, Assamese, English.
- Enrol only once — multiple enrolments cause rejection unless UIDAI advises otherwise.
- Waiting time for Aadhaar generation may be up to 90 days after the data packet is received.

## 2.3 Aadhaar Enrolment Centre
Available in Banks, Post Offices, BSNL Centres, CSCs, State Government offices, and dedicated
Aadhaar Seva Kendras (ASKs). ASKs are open all 7 days (except public holidays), 9:30 a.m.–5:30
p.m., handling 250–1000 requests/day.

**2.3.1** Locate a centre by calling 1947, via https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/easearch.aspx, or the
mAadhaar app.

**2.3.2 Types of centres:** (a) Registrar-run centres in banks/post offices/BSNL/CSC/state offices;
(b) UIDAI-run Aadhaar Seva Kendras (ASKs).

## 2.4 Online Appointment Service
Book an appointment for yourself, family or friend for: New Aadhaar enrolment, Name/Address/DOB/
Gender Update, Biometric (Photo+Fingerprints+Iris) Update, Mobile No. Update, Email ID Update.
The appointment acknowledgement (with Appointment ID and QR code) doubles as your
enrolment/update form — print it to save time (printing also available on-site).

**2.4.2 Booking process:** Visit appointments.uidai.gov.in/bookappointment.aspx → select centre →
"Proceed to Book Appointment" → choose New Aadhaar or Update → enter mobile number + captcha
→ enter OTP → fill and submit the displayed form → manage date/time via "Manage Appointment".

## 2.5 Aadhaar Generation Status
**2.5.1** If mobile/email was given at enrolment, you'll get SMS/email on generation status. Check via
UIDAI website or helpline 1947 using your EID (14-digit enrolment number + 14-digit timestamp on
your acknowledgement slip).

**2.5.2 Lost EID:** Retrieve online (registered mobile required — enter name, mobile, captcha, receive
OTP, log in, EID sent to registered mobile) or call helpline 1947.

# SECTION 3: AADHAAR UPDATE

You can update demographic details (name, address, DOB, gender, mobile, email) or biometrics
(photo, fingerprints, iris) at any Aadhaar Enrolment Centre nationwide. Multiple fields can be
updated in one visit at the same fee as a single field — so check all your details before visiting.

## 3.1 Documents Required for Update
Valid supporting document (in the applicant's name) required for: **Name, Address, Date of Birth**.
No document required for: **Gender, Mobile Number, Email ID, Photograph, Fingerprints & Iris
Scans, Local Language**.

Note: Father's/Mother's/Husband's Name in Aadhaar is part of the address field and can be
updated anytime (see 3.4).

## 3.2 Online Demographic Update Facility (SSUP)
Address (and other demographic fields, with document) can be updated online via the
Self-Service Update Portal: https://ssup.uidai.gov.in/ssup/. Registered mobile number required
for OTP login; otherwise visit an Enrolment Centre.

### 3.2.1 Process for online update with document
1. Visit SSUP → 2. Login with Aadhaar number → 3. Enter OTP → 4. Enter demographic details →
5. Allow/adjust regional-language transliteration → 6. Submit once satisfied → 7. Upload a clear
supporting document (pdf/png/jpeg; merge multi-page docs into one PDF) → 8. If original
unavailable, upload a copy attested by Notary Public or Gazetted Officer → 9. Review and edit if
needed → 10. Make payment → 11. Fee: Rs. 50/- per request (multiple fields in one request still
count as one request) → 12. Can cancel before UIDAI processing (refund on cancellation/payment
failure) → 13. Failed-payment requests can be cancelled after login.

Update limits noted here: DOB correction — 1 time; Name correction — 2 times; Gender
correction — 1 time.

### 3.2.2 Checkpoints to avoid rejection via SSUP
- Enter data matching the uploaded document exactly.
- Ensure the uploaded document bears the applicant's name.
- Select the correct document type matching what's uploaded.
- Use high-resolution images of original documents.
- Age on the document should match the Aadhaar photo; gender should match the photo.
- Don't submit incomplete requests.

## 3.3 Mandatory Biometric Update for Children
Children's biometrics (fingerprints, iris, photo) must be updated at age 5 and again at age 15.
Aadhaar number stays the same. **This update is FREE.**

## 3.4 Updating 'Care of' Field in Aadhaar Address
"C/o" (previously W/o, D/o, S/o) is part of the address field, not mandatory, and can be left blank
or updated via an address-update request (Enrolment Centre or SSUP). The supporting document
just needs to be in the applicant's own name.

## 3.5 Limits on Number of Updates
- **Name** — up to 2 times
- **Gender** — 1 time
- **Date of Birth** — 1 time
- No limit on: address, email, mobile number, or any biometric (photo/fingerprints/iris) updates.

## 3.6 Exception Process for Aadhaar Update
If you need to update name/gender/DOB beyond the permissible limit:
1. Visit an Enrolment Centre with valid supporting document.
2. Place an update request.
3. Send the URN to help@uidai.gov.in or call 1947 requesting an "exception update".
The case is referred to your UIDAI Regional Office for due diligence before processing.

## 3.7 Update in Case of Aadhaar Deactivation
Periodic quality checks may find discrepancies in documents/biometrics, causing temporary
deactivation (Aadhaar OTP/biometric authentication won't work). Steps:
1. Check the UIDAI email/SMS/letter or call 1947 for next steps.
2. If biometric-related, visit an Enrolment Centre to update biometrics.
3. If document-related, contact the relevant Regional Office after due diligence.

## 3.8 Aadhaar Update Status Check
Use the 8-digit URN (on the update acknowledgement slip) via: helpline 1947, help@uidai.gov.in,
uidai.gov.in, mAadhaar App, or (for online SSUP requests specifically)
https://ssup.uidai.gov.in/checkSSUPStatus/checkupdatestatus.

## 3.9 Points to Remember for Update at Enrolment Centre
1. Any Aadhaar holder can request an update.
2. Requires authentication (biometric at centre, or OTP for online address update); for under-5s,
   authentication is by the linked parent/guardian.
3. For under-18s, parent/guardian consent is required.
4. Lost update slip/URN → follow EID-retrieval process (2.5.1).
5. Rejected request → call 1947 for the reason, then correct before re-submitting.
6. Aadhaar shows 'Invalid' → call 1947.
7. If your Aadhaar letter shows only 'Year of Birth', do NOT update — just download the latest
   Aadhaar or order a reprint to get the full DOB.

## 3.10 Exit From Aadhaar
An Aadhaar holder may, on turning 18, apply to a UIDAI Regional Office for cancellation of their
Aadhaar number within 6 months of turning 18. Identity is established at the Regional Office in
person; cancellation completes within 3 months.

# SECTION 4: VALID LIST OF DOCUMENTS FOR ENROLMENT/UPDATE

A valid supporting document is mandatory for: Aadhaar Enrolment, Name Update, Address Update,
Date of Birth Update. (No document required to visit a centre for other demographic/biometric
updates.) NRI enrolment requires Indian Passport as mandatory POI; any other valid document
from the list can serve as POA.

- **4.1 POI** — at least one Proof of Identity document with photograph, per the valid list.
- **4.2 POA** — a POA document in your name (or use the Address Validation Letter service, 3.2.2).
- **4.3 DOB** — a DOB document in your name per the valid list.
- **4.4 POR** — required for HoF-based or child enrolment.
- **4.5 Certificate for Aadhaar Enrolment/Update** — a UIDAI standard-format certificate from a
  competent authority can serve as POI/POA/DOB/POR proof if no other listed document is
  available (see the "list_of_acceptable_supporting_documents" file for the full certificate format
  and instructions).

(The full POI/POA/POR/DOB document tables are in the companion file:
`list_of_acceptable_supporting_documents.md` — not duplicated here to avoid redundancy.)

# SECTION 6: GETTING AADHAAR

## 6.1–6.2 Aadhaar Generation & Letter
After generation or an update, the Aadhaar Letter is printed and posted to your registered address
via India Post; an SMS is sent if a mobile number was given. Call 1947 for dispatch date; contact
your local post office if not received within 15 days of dispatch.

## 6.3 Download eAadhaar
Download from https://eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/#/ or mAadhaar App using either:
- EID: 28-digit enrolment number + full name + PIN code, or
- UID: 12-digit Aadhaar number + full name + PIN code.

**6.3.1** OTP sent to registered mobile (or use Time-based OTP via mAadhaar App). Downloaded
Aadhaar (eAadhaar) is a password-protected PDF — password = first 4 letters of name (CAPITALS)
+ year of birth (YYYY).

**6.3.2** If the digital signature shows a '?' mark, you must validate it before use.

**6.3.3** eAadhaar is legally valid, same as the physical letter; verifiable via its QR code.

**6.3.4 Features:** improved layout, issue date = generation date, emblem + Aadhaar logo on both
sides, larger resident photo, digitally signed secure QR code with photo, VID printed below the
Aadhaar number.

## 6.4 mAadhaar Mobile App
Official app offering 35+ online Aadhaar services; available on Google Play and Apple App Store.

## 6.5 Aadhaar PVC Card
Durable card form with digitally signed secure QR code + photo + demographic details. Order
online via uidai.gov.in or resident.uidai.gov.in for Rs. 50/-. Security features: Secure QR Code,
Hologram, Micro text, Ghost image, Issue/Print Date, Guilloche Pattern, Embossed Aadhaar Logo.
Order using Aadhaar number, VID (16-digit), or EID (28-digit); pay via Credit/Debit Card, Net
Banking, or UPI; delivered by Speed Post with SMS tracking (SRN + AWB number).

# SECTION 7: VERIFICATION OF AADHAAR

## 7.1 Verify Any Aadhaar Online
Via uidai.gov.in, you (or anyone) can check whether an Aadhaar number is genuine and see: whether
it exists, age band, gender, state of residence, and mobile number (masked, last 4 digits only).
Methods: https://resident.uidai.gov.in/verify, mAadhaar App, or scanning the QR code on a
presented Aadhaar via the Aadhaar QR Code Scanner App/mAadhaar.

## 7.2 Secure QR Code
Present on the Aadhaar letter, PVC Card, and eAadhaar — digitally signed with demographic
details + photo, scannable for identity verification.

# SECTION 9: PRIVACY AND SECURITY OF AADHAAR DATA

## 9.1 UIDAI's Privacy Practices
- Collects only what's needed to issue/verify Aadhaar: name, DOB, gender, address + photo,
  fingerprints, iris. Mobile/email optional. Parent/guardian name required only for children.
- Does NOT collect religion, caste, community, class, ethnicity, income, or health status — no
  profiling possible from Aadhaar data.
- Enrolment/update uses UIDAI-certified devices only; data is encrypted immediately on capture.
- Verification responses are yes/no only — no personal data is revealed during authentication.
- The Aadhaar database is not linked to any other database; used only to verify identity at point
  of service, with the holder's consent.

## 9.2 Data Security in CIDR
Central Identities Data Repository (CIDR) is secured electronically and physically, with logged
access and strict security/storage protocols. Unauthorized access (including hacking/tampering)
carries severe penalties under the Aadhaar Act, 2016.

## 9.3 Further Self-Protection Options
- **Virtual ID (VID):** a 16-digit token usable instead of your Aadhaar number for authentication;
  generate via uidai.gov.in, mAadhaar App, or SMS.
- **Biometric Lock/Unlock:** temporarily lock/unlock your biometrics (OTP-authenticated).
- **Aadhaar Lock/Unlock:** lock your whole Aadhaar number to block all authentication methods;
  unlock only via VID (OTP-authenticated).

# SECTION 11: THE AADHAAR ACT 2016

UIDAI is a statutory authority under the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other
Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016, published in the official gazette on 26 March 2016 and
notified as an Authority on 12 July 2016. It functions under the Ministry of Electronics and
Information Technology (MeitY).

Regulations notified 14 September 2016: (1) Transaction of Business at Meetings of the Authority
Regulations; (2) Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) Regulations; (3) Aadhaar (Authentication)
Regulations; (4) Aadhaar (Data Security) Regulations; (5) Aadhaar (Sharing of Information)
Regulations.

**11.1 UIDAI's responsibilities:** operation/management of the entire Aadhaar life cycle; policy
development; procedures/systems for issuing Aadhaar numbers; authentication performance.

# SECTION 12: AADHAAR (ENROLMENT AND UPDATE) REGULATIONS, 2016 (Key Provisions)

## Chapter II — Resident Enrolment Process

**Reg. 3 — Biometric information for enrolment** (all individuals except under-5s): facial image; all
ten fingerprints; both iris scans. Standards as specified by the Authority.

**Reg. 4 — Demographic information for enrolment** (all individuals except under-5s): Name, DOB,
Gender, Residential Address (mandatory); Mobile number, Email address (optional). For
Introducer-based enrolment, additionally: Introducer's name and Aadhaar number. For HoF-based
enrolment, additionally: HoF's name, relationship, HoF's Aadhaar number, one biometric modality
of the HoF. **Demographic information shall NOT include race, religion, caste, tribe, ethnicity,
language, record of entitlement, income, or medical history.**

**Reg. 5 — Enrolment of children below five:** collect name, DOB, gender, EID/Aadhaar of one
parent (preferably mother if both alive) or guardian (mandatory, with relationship field), and the
child's address (same as the linked parent/guardian). Facial image of the child is captured; one
parent/guardian's biometrics captured/authenticated at enrolment. A PoR document establishing
the parent/guardian–child relationship is required; only children named in that PoR document
can be enrolled against it.

**Reg. 6 — Biometric exceptions:** where fingerprints can't be captured (injury, deformity,
amputation, etc.), only iris scans are taken. Where no biometric at all can be captured, the
Authority's exception-handling procedure applies.

## Chapter IV — Update of Resident Information

**Reg. 16:** An Aadhaar holder may seek alteration of demographic/biometric info per Section 31 of
the Act, following this chapter's procedure.

**Reg. 17 — Mandatory update for children:** biometrics must be updated on turning 5 and 15.

**Reg. 19 — Modes of updating:** (a) at any enrolment centre with operator/supervisor assistance,
biometrically authenticated, providing Aadhaar number + info to update; (b) online, for
demographic info, via designated portals with Aadhaar number + registered mobile OTP
authentication. An update ID is assigned for status tracking; a revised Aadhaar letter is issued per
Authority procedure.

**Reg. 20 — Convenience fee:** Registrars may be authorised to charge a fee (capped by the
Authority) for demographic/biometric updates.

## Chapter VI — Omission or Deactivation of Aadhaar Number

**Reg. 27 — Cancellation (omission) cases:** duplicate Aadhaar numbers (earliest retained, rest
cancelled); "Photo on Photo" enrolment without fresh core biometrics; falsely-claimed biometric
exception; an adult wrongly enrolled as a under-5 child to avoid biometrics; any other enrolment
the Authority deems fraudulent. Cancellation permanently disables all Authority-provided
services for that Aadhaar number.

**Reg. 28 — Deactivation cases (temporary, reversible on correction):** "Photo on Photo" where core
biometrics DO exist (deactivated until photo is updated, then reactivated); false partial biometric
exception; enrolment later found to lack valid supporting documents (deactivated until updated
with valid documents); data flagged as "bad" (mixed/anomalous biometrics, abusive language in
demographics, multiple names via 'urf'/'alias', etc.) until corrected; child failing to complete the
mandatory 5-year/15-year biometric update within 2 years of the due age (and omitted entirely if
still not done 1 year after deactivation); any other case the Authority deems appropriate.

**Reg. 29 — Inquiry:** possible omission/deactivation cases may involve a field inquiry (including
hearing the affected person); an Authority-nominated agency examines and reports; the Authority
decides.

**Reg. 30 — Communication:** the holder is informed of omission/deactivation and reasons via
SMS/registered email/tele-calling/letter; complaints go through the grievance redressal mechanism.

**Reg. 31 — Rectification:** omission (other than duplicate-Aadhaar cases) requires re-enrolment;
deactivation requires updating the identity information per Authority guidelines.
