**Notes (up-to-5 age band):**
- Document valid only if: currently valid; person is entitled to it; if publicly/digitally verifiable, verification is done; issuing authority hasn't disclaimed the document type.
- Birth certificate mandatory for Resident Indian/NRI born on/after 1.10.2023.
- HoF-based enrolment is mandatory for under-5s (except shelter-home/orphanage children and foreign nationals). Either parent or legal guardian can be HoF.
- HoF must have a valid Aadhaar before performing HoF-based enrolment; both parents' Aadhaar numbers required; biometric authentication by one parent mandatory.
- Child's name and HoF's name must both appear in the PoR document.
- Name is replicated exactly as in the supporting document — no added parent/guardian name parts.
- Address on child's Aadhaar = HoF's Aadhaar address.
- Foreign national's Aadhaar valid only till visa validity (Nepal/Bhutan: 10 years; OCI: 10 years; LTV: till LTV validity).
- HoF encouraged to provide any of: Indian passport, Photo ID/Certificate from Central/State Govt, ST/SC/OBC Certificate, Disability ID/Certificate.

## Certificate for Aadhaar Enrolment/Update (used only as Proof of Address, and as PoI in specific listed cases)

To be printed on plain A4 paper (not letterhead). Valid for 3 months from date of issue.

**Resident section fields:** Aadhaar Number (update only) · Full Name · C/o · House No./Bldg./Apt · Street/Road/Lane · Landmark · Area/Locality/Sector · Village/Town/City · Post Office · District · State · PIN Code · Date of Birth · Resident/NRI · New Enrolment/Update Request · Resident's photo (3.5cm x 4.5cm, cross-signed & cross-stamped) · Resident's signature/thumb impression.

**Certifier section fields:** Name of Certifier · Designation · Office Address · Contact Number · Certifier type (tick one): Gazetted Officer Group A, Village Panchayat Head/Mukhiya, Gazetted Officer Group B, MP/MLA/MLC/Municipal Councillor, Tehsildar, Head of Recognized Educational Institution, Superintendent/Warden/Matron/Head of shelter home/orphanage, EPFO Officer · Signature & Stamp of Certifier.

**Certifier checklist:** No overwriting · Issue date filled · Resident's signature present · Certifier's details complete · Resident's photo cross-signed and cross-stamped.

**Key filling instructions:**
- Use CAPITAL LETTERS, standard fonts, black/blue ballpoint pen only (no ink-pen or pencil).
- Tick only relevant option boxes; leave others blank.
- Do not write "NA"/"N/A"/"NOT APPLICABLE" — leave irrelevant fields blank.
- Certificate must be submitted within 3 months of the date of issue, or the enrolment/update request will be rejected.
- For Enrolment, leave Aadhaar Number blank; for Update, it is mandatory.
- This document is valid as Proof of Address; usable as Proof of Identity only in specific cases listed in the main supporting-document tables above.
