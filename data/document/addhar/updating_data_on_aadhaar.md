# Updating Data on Aadhaar — Official Process Summary

Source: UIDAI, "Updating Data on Aadhaar"
https://uidai.gov.in/en/updating-data-on-aadhaar
(Content last updated by UIDAI: 17 Sep 2026)

## What can be updated

| Category | Fields |
|---|---|
| Demographic | Name, Address, Date of Birth/Age, Gender, Mobile Number, Email Address, Relationship status, Information-sharing consent |
| Biometric | Iris, Fingerprints, Facial Photograph |

## Why an update is usually needed

- Life events (marriage, relocation, death of a relative) changing name, address, mobile number, or related-person details.
- Corrections to errors made at the time of original enrolment (commonly DOB/Age and Gender).
- Changing the local language originally used to record demographic details.
- UIDAI itself flagging that a person's on-file POI/POA documents are outdated or of poor quality, and asking the holder to re-submit.

## Why a biometric update is needed

- **Under 5 at first enrolment:** must be re-enrolled with full biometrics at age 5. This is treated as a fresh enrolment request while keeping the same Aadhaar number.
- **5–15 at enrolment:** must submit full biometrics again on turning 15.
- **Above 15 at enrolment:** recommended to refresh biometrics every 10 years.
- Accident/illness causing a biometric exception.
- Repeated authentication failures (false rejects) traceable to poor-quality captured biometrics.
- UIDAI-side quality checks flagging biometrics below the acceptable threshold.

## Modes of update

### 1. Online (self-service, address only)
- Done via the myAadhaar portal.
- Requires a mobile number already registered with the Aadhaar.
- Login by Aadhaar number + OTP.
- Applicant uploads supporting Proof of Address (POA) documents; UIDAI's back-office verification team checks these afterward.
- Only the **address** field can be changed through this online route.

### 2. At an Aadhaar Enrolment/Update Centre (assisted)
Needed for all other demographic fields and for any biometric update. An operator enters the request and a verifier checks the supporting documents on the spot. Two operator-side modes exist:

**a. Update Client Standard**
- Can update all biometric and demographic fields, including local language.
- Identity check: biometric authentication at the back-end.
- Document verification for any field that requires documentary evidence, done by a UIDAI/Registrar-appointed verifier.
- Applicant receives an acknowledgement with a trackable Update Request Number (URN).

**b. Update Client Lite (UCL)**
- Can update demographic fields and photo (including local language), but not other biometrics.
- Identity check: biometric authentication of the applicant themself.
- Same document-verification standard as Standard mode (per DDSVP Committee recommendations).
- Applicant receives an acknowledgement with an Enrolment ID (EID) usable for status tracking.

## Registered mobile number
A mobile number linked to Aadhaar is required to use any Aadhaar online service (including the online address-update route). If no mobile number was registered at enrolment, the holder must visit a Permanent Enrolment Centre to register one — it cannot be added purely online.
