# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

## Overview
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded health assurance scheme, providing free secondary and tertiary healthcare coverage of up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for hospitalization. It is aimed at economically vulnerable families identified through the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 database.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Families identified as deprived in rural areas based on SECC 2011 deprivation criteria (e.g., households with no adult member between 16-59 years, female-headed households with no adult male member, households with disabled member and no able-bodied adult, SC/ST households, landless households deriving income from manual labor).
- Occupational categories in urban areas (e.g., rag pickers, domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers, sanitation workers, home-based artisans, and similar informal occupations) as per SECC 2011 urban criteria.
- No cap on family size, age, or gender for eligible families.
- Coverage can also be extended through state-specific portability or top-up schemes in some states.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card (for identity verification).
- Ration card or SECC verification document (to confirm inclusion in the beneficiary database).
- Mobile number for OTP verification.
- Family ID / household details matching SECC records.

## How to Apply / Check Eligibility
1. Check eligibility online via the official PM-JAY website (pmjay.gov.in) or mAyushman app by entering mobile number, ration card number, or name/location search.
2. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or empaneled hospital's Ayushman Mitra help desk with Aadhaar and supporting documents for verification.
3. Once eligibility is confirmed, an Ayushman Card (e-card) is generated, which must be presented at any empaneled hospital for cashless treatment.

There is no "application" in the traditional sense since eligibility is pre-determined by SECC data; the process is primarily verification and card generation.

## Benefits
- Coverage up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year, on a floater basis (shared among all members).
- Covers pre and post-hospitalization expenses, medicines, diagnostics, and over 1,900 medical packages/procedures.
- Cashless and paperless treatment at empaneled public and private hospitals across India.
- No restriction on family size, age, or existing medical conditions (pre-existing diseases covered from day one).
- Portable across India - beneficiaries can avail treatment at any empaneled hospital in any state.

## Processing Time
Eligibility verification and Ayushman Card generation typically takes a few minutes to a few hours if done at a CSC or hospital help desk with correct documents. In case of database mismatches, resolution may take a few days through the local grievance redressal mechanism.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Name or date of birth mismatch between Aadhaar and SECC database records.
- Assuming eligibility without checking the official portal - not all economically weak families are automatically covered; it strictly depends on SECC 2011 criteria.
- Not generating the e-card in advance, causing delays during medical emergencies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a premium or fee to enroll in Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY?**
A: No, the scheme is entirely free for eligible beneficiaries; there is no premium or enrollment fee.

**Q: How do I find out if my family is covered under PM-JAY?**
A: You can check eligibility on the official PM-JAY website or mAyushman app using your mobile number, name, or ration card details.

**Q: Can I get treatment at a private hospital under this scheme?**
A: Yes, as long as the private hospital is empaneled under PM-JAY; treatment at empaneled hospitals (public or private) is cashless.

**Q: What happens if my family is not listed in the SECC database but is genuinely poor?**
A: Some states have launched extension schemes to cover additional families; you should check with your state health department or local CSC for state-specific extension schemes.

**Q: Does PM-JAY cover outpatient (OPD) treatment?**
A: No, PM-JAY primarily covers secondary and tertiary hospitalization expenses; routine OPD consultations are generally not covered under the core scheme.
