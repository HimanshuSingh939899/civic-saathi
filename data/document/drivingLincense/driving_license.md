# Driving License

## Overview
A Driving License (DL) is an official document issued by the Regional Transport Office (RTO) / State Transport Department authorizing an individual to operate motor vehicles on public roads in India. It is governed under the Motor Vehicles Act and managed largely through the Parivahan Sewa portal.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum age of 18 years for a license to drive a motor vehicle (gearless two-wheelers up to 50cc can be driven from age 16 with parental/guardian consent and a specific learner's license category).
- Must pass a learner's license test (basic traffic rules and signs) before applying for a permanent license.
- Must pass a driving skill test conducted by the RTO for the permanent license.
- Must not be disqualified from holding a license due to prior offences, medical unfitness, or court orders.
- Medical fitness certificate required for certain categories (e.g., transport/commercial vehicle licenses, or applicants above a certain age).

## Documents Required
- Proof of age: Birth certificate, 10th marksheet, PAN card, or passport.
- Proof of address: Aadhaar card, utility bill, rent agreement, voter ID, or passport.
- Passport-size photographs.
- Medical certificate (Form 1A) for transport vehicle licenses or applicants above 40/50 years (age threshold varies by state) applying for renewal.
- Learner's License (LL) number and details (required before applying for permanent DL).

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Learner's License (LL)
1. Register on the Parivahan Sewa portal (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) or respective state RTO portal.
2. Fill the learner's license application form and upload required documents.
3. Book a slot for the online learner's license test (multiple-choice questions on traffic rules, signs, and road safety).
4. Upon passing, the Learner's License is issued, valid for 6 months.

### Step 2: Permanent Driving License
1. After holding the LL for a minimum period (typically 30 days, varies by state), apply for the permanent DL online.
2. Book a slot for the driving skill test at the RTO.
3. Attend the RTO in person for the driving test (on a two-wheeler, four-wheeler, or relevant vehicle category test track).
4. Upon passing, the permanent DL is issued, typically dispatched by post to the registered address.

## Vehicle Categories
- MC 50CC (gearless two-wheeler, non-transport).
- MCWG (Motorcycle With Gear).
- LMV (Light Motor Vehicle - cars, jeeps).
- Transport categories (commercial vehicles, requiring additional medical and training certification).

## Processing Time
- Learner's License: Typically issued same-day or within 1-2 days of passing the online test.
- Permanent License: Usually issued within 7-15 days after passing the driving test at the RTO, though this can vary by state and RTO workload.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Applying for the permanent license before the mandatory minimum gap period after obtaining the learner's license.
- Address mismatch between Aadhaar/address proof and the application form.
- Missing the driving test slot, which may require rebooking and additional waiting time.
- Not renewing the license before expiry, which can lead to penalty fees or requirement of a fresh test in case of significant delay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the validity period of a permanent driving license?**
A: Non-transport licenses are typically valid for 20 years or until the holder turns 50, whichever is earlier, after which renewal is required every 5-10 years depending on age; transport licenses generally have shorter validity (commonly 3 years) and require medical certification for renewal.

**Q: Can I apply for a driving license in a state different from my permanent address?**
A: Yes, you can apply based on your current residential address with valid address proof, even if it differs from your permanent/home state address.

**Q: Is the driving test mandatory even if I already know how to drive?**
A: Yes, the driving skill test at the RTO is mandatory for all first-time permanent license applicants, regardless of prior driving experience.

**Q: What happens if I fail the driving test?**
A: You can reschedule and retake the test after a specified waiting period (varies by state, commonly 7 days), usually with an additional fee.

**Q: Can I add another vehicle category to my existing license later?**
A: Yes, you can apply to add additional vehicle class/category (e.g., adding four-wheeler to an existing two-wheeler license) through a separate application and test process at the RTO.
