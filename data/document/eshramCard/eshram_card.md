# e-Shram Card (National Database of Unorganised Workers)

## Overview
e-Shram is a national database of unorganised workers, launched by the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India. It aims to register workers in the informal sector (construction workers, migrant laborers, domestic workers, street vendors, agricultural workers, gig and platform workers, etc.) and provide them access to various social security schemes and benefits.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Must be an unorganised sector worker (not a member of EPFO or ESIC, and not a government employee paying income tax).
- Age between 16 and 59 years.
- Must be an Indian citizen.
- Includes workers such as: construction workers, migrant workers, domestic workers, agricultural laborers, street vendors, rickshaw pullers, rag pickers, gig/platform workers (delivery partners, cab drivers), and home-based workers.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card (mandatory).
- Aadhaar-linked mobile number (for OTP verification). If mobile number is not linked, applicant must visit a Common Service Centre (CSC).
- Bank account details (account number and IFSC code) for linking benefits.
- Basic details: occupation, education level, skill type, monthly income, family details.

## How to Apply
1. **Online Self-Registration**: Visit the official eShram portal (eshram.gov.in), enter Aadhaar-linked mobile number, verify via OTP, and fill in personal, occupational, and bank details.
2. **Through Common Service Centre (CSC)**: Workers without Aadhaar-linked mobile numbers can visit the nearest CSC with their Aadhaar card for biometric-based registration.
3. **Through State Seva Kendras / Labour Facilitation Centres**: Many states have set up help desks to assist with registration.

Registration is free of cost at government portals and CSCs.

## Benefits of Registration
- Unique 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) issued.
- Automatic enrollment in Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) providing accidental insurance cover (death/disability coverage).
- Access to government welfare schemes directly linked to the e-Shram database (varies by state and central scheme updates).
- Portability: the UAN remains valid across India regardless of change in employment or location.
- Used by government for targeted delivery of benefits during emergencies (e.g., pandemic relief, disaster relief).

## Processing Time
Registration via the online portal is typically instant; the e-Shram card (UAN) is generated immediately upon successful submission of details. CSC-based registration may take 1-2 days for data processing.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Aadhaar details not matching the name/date of birth entered during registration.
- Entering incorrect bank account/IFSC details, which can delay benefit transfers.
- Not updating the account if the mobile number linked to Aadhaar changes later.
- Duplicate registration using different mobile numbers (only one UAN is allowed per individual).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is e-Shram registration mandatory for unorganised workers?**
A: While not legally mandatory, it is strongly recommended as it is the gateway to accessing various social security benefits and insurance coverage.

**Q: Can I register if I don't have a bank account?**
A: Bank account details are recommended for receiving monetary benefits, but basic registration can proceed with limited functionality; it is best to open a bank account (e.g., via Jan Dhan Yojana) before or during registration.

**Q: Is there an age limit for e-Shram registration?**
A: Yes, applicants must be between 16 and 59 years of age.

**Q: Can government employees or EPFO/ESIC members register?**
A: No, e-Shram is specifically for unorganised sector workers; those covered under EPFO, ESIC, or who are government employees are not eligible.

**Q: How do I check my e-Shram registration status?**
A: You can check status and download your e-Shram card using your UAN number on the official eshram.gov.in portal.
