# Income Certificate (आय प्रमाण पत्र) — Uttar Pradesh e-Saathi / e-District Portal

## Overview

An Income Certificate is an official document issued by the Uttar Pradesh Revenue Department that certifies the annual income of an individual or their family from all sources — salary, business, agriculture, pension, rent, and other income. It is issued after verification by the local revenue machinery (Lekhpal at the field level, approved by the Tehsildar).

- **Issuing authority:** Tehsildar, after an inspection report from the Lekhpal.
- **Applies to:** Any resident of Uttar Pradesh, for themselves or their family. There is no minimum or maximum income limit to apply — the certificate simply records the verified income figure.
- **Portal:** e-Sathi / e-District UP — https://edistrict.up.gov.in/edistrictup/ (citizen login is labelled "Citizen Login (e-Sathi)")
- **Also available at:** Common Service Centres (CSC) / Jan Seva Kendra, or offline at the local Tehsil office.

## Common Uses

- Scholarships and fee concessions in educational institutions
- EWS (Economically Weaker Section) and other income-based reservation claims
- Government pension schemes
- Applying for certain government jobs
- Subsidies (e.g., LPG) and other welfare schemes
- Loan applications at concessional interest rates

**Note:** An Income Certificate is not the same as:
- An **EWS income and assets certificate** (a separate certificate for EWS quota)
- An **OBC non-creamy layer certificate** (tests income plus parental status)
- A **Solvency Certificate / हैसियत प्रमाण पत्र** (certifies assets owned, not income; has a higher fee — ₹120 vs ₹30 for the income certificate)

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a resident of Uttar Pradesh.
- Income is assessed for the whole family/household, not just the individual applicant (a student applying for a scholarship, for example, declares household income, not just their own).
- The applicant must state their job/profession details and the specific purpose for which the certificate is needed — both are mandatory fields in the application.
- Income must be declared honestly; a certificate obtained on a false declaration can be cancelled and can invite action against the applicant, since the declared income is verified through the Lekhpal/Tehsil-level revenue machinery.

## Required Documents

1. **Aadhaar Card** — mandatory for e-KYC. In Uttar Pradesh, the income certificate service (like caste and domicile certificates) can only be filed after successful Aadhaar e-KYC. The name on Aadhaar must match the applicant's name exactly.
2. **Alternate photo ID** (only if Aadhaar is genuinely unavailable) — PAN card, Passport, Driving Licence, or Voter ID.
3. **Self-Declaration Form (स्वप्रमाणित घोषणा पत्र)** — a prescribed self-certified declaration of annual family income, downloaded from the portal, filled, signed, and uploaded as a scanned copy. This replaces a notarized affidavit.
4. **Residential/address proof** — Ration card copy, Voter ID, Electricity bill, Water bill, or Telephone bill.
5. **Proof of income:**
   - Salaried applicants: latest salary slip(s) (commonly the last 3 months on company letterhead); Form 16 or ITR if available.
   - Others: the self-declaration form serves as the primary income proof.
6. **Passport-size photograph** — captured live via webcam at the time of application/verification, or uploaded; it should match the applicant's Aadhaar photo.
7. Previous income certificate copy, if one was issued earlier (where applicable).

### File specifications for online upload
- Photograph: JPEG, generally under 50 KB.
- Other documents: JPEG or PDF, generally under 100 KB.
- Documents must be clear and legible; blurred or unreadable scans are a common reason for rejection at verification.

## Online Application Process (e-Sathi / e-District UP)

1. Go to the official portal: **https://edistrict.up.gov.in/edistrictup/**. Use only this official domain — fraudulent look-alike sites exist.
2. Click **"Citizen Login (e-Sathi)"** on the homepage.
3. **New users:** click **"New User Registration"**, fill in personal details (name, mobile number, email, district, date of birth), complete the CAPTCHA, and submit. An OTP is sent to the registered mobile number to verify the account; a password is then set/received.
4. **Log in** using the User ID, password, and CAPTCHA.
5. Select **"e-Sathi Integrated Services"**, then navigate to **Revenue Department → Income Certificate ("Aay Praman Patra")**.
6. Complete **Aadhaar e-KYC** — this is mandatory before the income certificate form can be filled.
7. Fill in the application form: applicant details, job/profession, family income details (from all sources and all family members), and the purpose for which the certificate is required.
8. Upload the required documents (ID proof, address proof, self-declaration, photograph, income proof) as per the file-size specifications above.
9. Pay the applicable **user charge** (commonly cited as ₹15 for the online user charge, with a separate ₹30 service fee line also listed on the portal's fee schedule) through the online payment gateway.
10. Submit the application and note the **Application/Acknowledgement Number** for tracking.
11. **Verification:** The Tehsildar forwards the application to the Lekhpal for field-level verification of the declared income and residence. Based on the Lekhpal's report, the Tehsildar approves and issues the certificate.
12. **Download:** Once approved, download the certificate from the e-Sathi portal, or retrieve it via DigiLocker.

### Offline Alternative
Applicants may instead visit the Tehsil / local Revenue Office or a Common Service Centre (CSC) / Jan Seva Kendra, submit the physical application form along with the same set of supporting documents, and receive a reference/record number for tracking. A physical/original self-declaration may still need to be submitted by hand or post to the concerned SDM/Tehsildar in some cases even after an online submission.

## Tracking the Application

1. Visit the e-District UP portal.
2. Click **"Track Application Status"**.
3. Enter the application number.
4. Click **"Search"** to view the current status.

## Timeline and Fees

- **Standard processing time:** Typically 3–7 working days as per the e-District UP service standard for Income/Caste/Domicile certificates, though the broader published time limit for Income Certificate in UP is cited as up to 20 days.
- **Fee:** ₹15 online user charge when self-applying via e-Sathi (a ₹30 service fee line is also listed on the portal's fee schedule); CSC/Jan Seva Kendra may charge an additional facilitation fee.

## Validity

Uttar Pradesh's e-District portal does not print a fixed validity period on the Income Certificate itself; income can change year to year, so the requesting institution/scheme (school, scholarship authority, government department) decides how recent a certificate it will accept. In practice, certificates are often treated as valid for about **one to three years**, but the specific scheme's notice should always be checked, and a fresh certificate obtained if a more recent one is required. (For comparison, Bihar's RTPS portal explicitly states a one-year validity for its income certificate — UP does not publish an equivalent fixed figure.)

## Common Rejection Reasons / Errors

- Aadhaar e-KYC not completed or Aadhaar details (especially name) not matching the application — the service will not proceed without successful e-KYC.
- Salaried applicant fails to attach the latest salary slip.
- Declared income figure does not match supporting documents (salary slip/ITR), raising questions at Lekhpal verification.
- Missing or illegible required attachments — an application found incomplete at verification is rejected and cannot be revived; a fresh application must be filed.
- Application stuck beyond the standard time limit — applicant should contact the approving authority (Tehsildar) in their district.
- Certificate not verifying online — original documents may need to be presented at the district administration/approving authority's office.

## Certificate Verification

Anyone (e.g., an educational institution or scheme authority) can verify an issued certificate's authenticity through the certificate verification section of the e-District UP portal, typically by entering the certificate/application number.

## Grievance / Helpline

- **Uttar Pradesh e-District Helpline:** 0522-2304706 (Monday–Saturday, 10 am–5 pm, except government holidays)
- Grievances can also be registered through the "Register Grievance" section on the e-Sathi/e-District UP portal.
