# Passport (Passport Seva)

## Overview
A passport is an official travel document issued by the Government of India, certifying the identity and nationality of the holder for the purpose of international travel. The Passport Seva Programme, run by the Ministry of External Affairs, manages issuance, renewal, and related services across India through Passport Seva Kendras (PSKs) and Regional Passport Offices (RPOs).

## Eligibility Criteria
- Must be an Indian citizen (by birth, descent, registration, or naturalization, as applicable).
- No specific age restriction; separate categories exist for minors (below 18 years) and adults.
- Applicants with pending criminal cases or certain legal restrictions may face additional scrutiny or denial, subject to police verification outcomes.

## Documents Required
- Proof of date of birth: Birth certificate, 10th-class marksheet/certificate with DOB, or PAN card.
- Proof of address: Aadhaar card, utility bill, bank passbook, rent agreement, or similar document not older than specified validity (varies by document type).
- Proof of identity: Aadhaar card, Voter ID, or similar government-issued photo ID.
- For minors: birth certificate and parents' passport copies (if available) or applicable proof.
- Passport-size photographs as per prescribed specifications (for certain application types; many steps are now digitized with photo capture at the PSK).
- Annexures/affidavits as applicable (e.g., for name change, address proof annexure, or specific family situations).

## How to Apply
1. Register on the Passport Seva Online Portal and create a user account.
2. Fill the online application form (fresh issue, reissue/renewal, or other services like police clearance certificate, tatkal, etc.).
3. Pay the applicable fee online.
4. Schedule an appointment at the nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK).
5. Visit the PSK/POPSK on the scheduled date with original documents for verification, biometric capture, and photograph.
6. Police verification is conducted (pre-police verification for fresh applicants in most cases, or post-police verification depending on application category).
7. Passport is printed and dispatched by post/speed post upon successful verification.

## Application Types
- **Fresh/Normal**: Standard processing, typically 30-45 days including police verification.
- **Tatkal**: Expedited service with higher fees; processing can be completed in a few days, though police verification may still occur post-issuance in some cases.
- **Reissue/Renewal**: For passport expiry, exhaustion of pages, or changes in personal particulars.
- **Diplomatic/Official**: For government officials, processed through separate channels.

## Processing Time
- Normal application: Approximately 30-45 days (including police verification), though this can vary by city and case specifics.
- Tatkal application: Typically 1-3 working days for printing/dispatch after document verification, subject to no adverse police report.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Address proof document that does not match current residential address exactly.
- Name mismatch across different submitted documents (e.g., spelling variations).
- Incomplete or incorrect annexure forms for special cases (e.g., name change, single parent, etc.).
- Missing or delayed response to police verification visit, which can significantly delay processing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the validity period of a standard Indian passport?**
A: Passports issued to adults are generally valid for 10 years; passports issued to minors (below 18 years) are valid for 5 years or until the minor turns 18, whichever is earlier.

**Q: Can I apply for a passport without Aadhaar?**
A: Yes, Aadhaar is not mandatory, but it simplifies the application process significantly; alternative documents for address and identity proof are accepted as per the official document list.

**Q: What is the difference between ECR and Non-ECR passports?**
A: ECR (Emigration Check Required) passports require emigration clearance for travel to certain countries for employment purposes, typically applicable to those who have not completed education beyond a certain level; Non-ECR passports do not have this requirement.

**Q: How do I track my passport application status?**
A: You can track the status online on the Passport Seva portal using your application reference number (file number).

**Q: Is police verification mandatory for all passport applications?**
A: Police verification is mandatory for most fresh applications, though in some cases, pre-verified applicants (such as government employees or those with sufficient prior documentation) may be exempted or undergo post-issuance verification.
