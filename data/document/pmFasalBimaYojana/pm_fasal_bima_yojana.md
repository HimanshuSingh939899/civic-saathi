# PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) - Crop Insurance Scheme

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana is a government-backed crop insurance scheme designed to provide financial support to farmers in the event of crop failure due to natural calamities, pests, or diseases. It aims to stabilize farmers' income and encourage adoption of modern agricultural practices.

## Eligibility Criteria
- All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas during the relevant season are eligible, including:
  - Loanee farmers (those who have taken crop loans from financial institutions for notified crops) - enrollment was earlier compulsory but is now voluntary.
  - Non-loanee farmers (those who have not taken crop loans) can also enroll voluntarily.
- Both landowner farmers and tenant/sharecropper farmers cultivating notified crops are eligible.
- Coverage is available for food crops (cereals, millets, pulses), oilseeds, and annual commercial/horticultural crops as notified by the respective state government for each season.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card.
- Land ownership documents (land records/khasra-khatauni) for landowner farmers, or tenancy/cultivation agreement for tenant farmers.
- Bank account details (for premium payment and claim settlement via DBT).
- Sowing certificate or self-declaration of crop sown (specifying crop type and area under cultivation).
- Passport-size photograph.

## How to Apply
1. **For loanee farmers**: Enrollment can be facilitated automatically through the bank at the time of crop loan disbursement, though farmers now have the option to opt out if they do not wish to be covered.
2. **For non-loanee farmers**: Apply online through the National Crop Insurance Portal (pmfby.gov.in), or through Common Service Centres (CSCs), insurance company agents, or designated bank branches.
3. Submit required documents including land records, Aadhaar, bank details, and crop sowing details before the cutoff date for the respective season (Kharif or Rabi).
4. Pay the farmer's share of the premium (balance is subsidized by central and state governments).

## Premium Structure
- Farmers pay a nominal premium: 2% of sum insured for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5% for annual commercial/horticultural crops.
- The remaining premium amount is subsidized and shared between the central and state governments.

## Benefits
- Comprehensive risk coverage from pre-sowing to post-harvest losses, including coverage for prevented sowing, standing crop losses due to natural calamities, post-harvest losses (up to a specified period for crops kept in the field after harvesting), and localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation).
- Use of technology (satellite imagery, drones, smartphones) for faster and more accurate yield estimation and claim settlement.
- Claims are directly credited to the farmer's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Processing Time
Claim settlement timelines vary based on the type of loss assessment (yield-based vs. localized/individual farm-level assessment) and the time needed for Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) conducted at harvest; claims are typically processed within a few weeks to a couple of months after final yield data is submitted by state authorities, though delays can occur depending on state-level data submission timelines.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Missing the enrollment cutoff date for the respective crop season (Kharif/Rabi), after which new enrollment is not possible for that season.
- Incorrect or mismatched land records/crop details, which can lead to claim rejection or delay.
- Bank account not properly linked with Aadhaar, causing delays in claim disbursement.
- Not reporting crop loss within the stipulated time window (typically 72 hours) for localized calamities like hailstorm or landslide, which requires individual farmer-level intimation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is PMFBY compulsory for farmers who have taken agricultural loans?**
A: No, enrollment is now voluntary for all farmers, including loanee farmers, who can opt out of the scheme by submitting a declaration within the prescribed timeline before the season's cutoff.

**Q: How is the crop loss assessed for claim settlement?**
A: Loss assessment is primarily based on Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) conducted at the Gram Panchayat/notified unit level to estimate actual yield compared to the threshold yield; for localized calamities, individual farm-level assessment is conducted upon farmer intimation.

**Q: What is the deadline to report crop damage due to a localized calamity like a hailstorm?**
A: Farmers must report such localized crop damage within 72 hours of the event through the toll-free helpline, mobile app, or to the concerned insurance company/bank/agriculture department office.

**Q: Can a farmer insure crops on leased or rented land?**
A: Yes, tenant farmers and sharecroppers cultivating notified crops on leased land are eligible, provided they can furnish valid tenancy/cultivation agreements or documents as required by the state.

**Q: Does PMFBY cover losses due to a farmer's own negligence?**
A: No, the scheme covers losses due to defined perils such as natural calamities, pests, and diseases; losses arising from preventable factors or negligence are generally excluded as per the scheme's terms and conditions.
