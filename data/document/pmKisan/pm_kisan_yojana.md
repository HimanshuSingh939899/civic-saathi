# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## Overview
PM-KISAN is a central sector scheme providing direct income support to landholding farmer families across India. Eligible farmers receive a fixed cash benefit directly in their bank accounts to help meet agricultural and other input costs.

## Eligibility Criteria
- All landholding farmer families with cultivable land, subject to the exclusion criteria below, are eligible, irrespective of the size of landholding.
- Land records must be in the name of the farmer/family as per state land records.
- **Excluded categories** (not eligible for PM-KISAN):
  - Institutional landholders.
  - Farmer families with one or more members who are/were constitutional post holders, former/present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors, or District Panchayat chairpersons.
  - Serving or retired government employees (Group A/B officers; some exceptions for Group C/D or multi-tasking staff in certain cases).
  - Income tax payees in the last assessment year.
  - Professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, and architects practicing professionally.
  - Pensioners drawing monthly pension of Rs. 10,000 or more (excluding Group C/D or MTS staff).

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card (mandatory, with few exceptions for certain states/UTs at initial rollout).
- Land ownership records/documents (khasra-khatauni or equivalent as per state).
- Bank account details (account number and IFSC code) linked with Aadhaar for Direct Benefit Transfer.
- Mobile number for OTP verification and status updates.

## How to Apply
1. Apply online through the official PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in) under the "New Farmer Registration" option, entering Aadhaar number and other details.
2. Alternatively, apply through the local Common Service Centre (CSC), with applicable CSC service charges.
3. Can also apply through the State Nodal Officer, Patwari, or Agriculture Department official in some states, who verify land records.
4. After submission, the application undergoes verification by state/local revenue and agriculture department officials before approval.
5. Once approved, the farmer is added to the beneficiary list, and installments are credited via DBT.

## Benefit Structure
- Eligible farmer families receive Rs. 6,000 per year, paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 each, credited directly to their bank accounts every four months.
- Funds are transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), with no intermediary involvement.

## Processing Time
New registrations typically take a few weeks for verification by local revenue/agriculture authorities; the exact time varies by state due to differences in land record digitization and verification processes. Once approved, subsequent installments are released as per the scheme's regular disbursement cycle.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Aadhaar details (name, date of birth) not matching bank account or land record details, which is a common reason for payment failure or rejection.
- Bank account not linked with Aadhaar, or incorrect IFSC code entry, delaying DBT credit.
- Land records not updated or in a different family member's name than the applicant, causing verification issues.
- Not completing mandatory eKYC (OTP-based, biometric, or face authentication via the PM-KISAN mobile app), which is required periodically to continue receiving installments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much financial assistance does a farmer receive under PM-KISAN annually?**
A: Eligible farmer families receive Rs. 6,000 per year, paid in three installments of Rs. 2,000 each, every four months.

**Q: Is PM-KISAN applicable to tenant farmers who do not own land?**
A: No, PM-KISAN is primarily for landholding farmer families whose land records are in their name; tenant farmers without land ownership documents are generally not eligible under the core scheme criteria.

**Q: What is eKYC and why is it required for PM-KISAN?**
A: eKYC is a mandatory identity verification step (via OTP, biometric, or face authentication) required to confirm the farmer's identity and continue receiving installments; without completing eKYC, future installments may be withheld.

**Q: How can I check my PM-KISAN payment status?**
A: You can check your beneficiary and payment status online on the PM-KISAN portal using your Aadhaar number, account number, or mobile number under the "Beneficiary Status" section.

**Q: Can income tax payers receive PM-KISAN benefits?**
A: No, farmer families where any member has paid income tax in the last assessment year are excluded from the scheme.

**Q: What should I do if my PM-KISAN installment is not credited?**
A: You should check your beneficiary status on the portal for error details (such as Aadhaar-bank mismatch or pending eKYC), and if needed, contact the local Agriculture Department office or PM-KISAN helpline to correct the issue.
