# Ration Card (Public Distribution System - PDS)

## Overview
A Ration Card is an official document issued by state governments that entitles households to purchase subsidized food grains and essential commodities through the Public Distribution System (PDS)/Fair Price Shops. It also serves as a widely accepted proof of identity and address, and is often required for enrollment in other welfare schemes (such as Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY eligibility verification).

## Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a resident of the state where the application is being made (ration cards are issued at the state level, not centrally).
- Household should not already possess a ration card in another state or already hold multiple ration cards.
- Categorized based on economic status:
  - **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY)**: For the poorest of the poor households.
  - **Below Poverty Line (BPL) / Priority Household (PHH)**: For households below the state-defined poverty line, eligible for subsidized food grains under the National Food Security Act (NFSA).
  - **Above Poverty Line (APL) / Non-Priority Household (NPHH)**: For households above the poverty line, eligible for a ration card but with lesser or no subsidy depending on state policy.
- Specific income and asset criteria for BPL/AAY categorization vary by state.

## Documents Required
- Aadhaar card of all family members.
- Proof of address: utility bill, rent agreement, or property documents.
- Proof of income/economic status (for BPL/AAY category classification), such as income certificate.
- Passport-size photographs of head of household (and sometimes all adult members).
- Bank account details (in some states, for linkage with DBT of food subsidies).
- Surrender certificate or proof of not holding an existing ration card elsewhere (self-declaration).

## How to Apply
1. Apply online through the respective state's Food, Civil Supplies, and Consumer Affairs Department portal, or through the National Food Security Portal where applicable.
2. Alternatively, apply offline at the local Fair Price Shop, Tehsil office, or designated ration card issuing center.
3. Submit required documents along with the application form.
4. Field verification may be conducted by a local Food & Supplies Inspector to confirm household details and economic status.
5. Upon approval, the ration card (physical or digital, depending on the state) is issued.

## Benefits
- Subsidized food grains (rice, wheat, sugar, kerosene in some states) under NFSA, with quantity and price varying by card category (AAY households typically receive the highest subsidy).
- One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) portability: allows beneficiaries to access their food grain entitlement from any Fair Price Shop across India, not just their registered location (useful for migrant workers).
- Acts as valid proof of identity and address for various other government and private services.
- Often linked as an eligibility document for schemes like Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, PM Awas Yojana, and various state welfare schemes.

## Processing Time
Typically takes 2-4 weeks from application submission to issuance, including field verification, though this can vary significantly by state and category applied for.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Applying for a new ration card without surrendering an existing one from a previous address/state, which can lead to rejection or legal issues (duplicate ration cards are against regulations).
- Mismatched family member details between Aadhaar and the ration card application.
- Incorrect income/category declaration, which may be flagged during verification and could affect eligibility for the applied category.
- Not updating the ration card after changes in family composition (birth, marriage, death) or address.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)?**
A: ONORC is a portability initiative allowing ration card holders, especially migrant workers, to access their entitled food grains from any Fair Price Shop in India using the same ration card, instead of being restricted to their registered location.

**Q: How do I add a new family member (e.g., newborn) to my ration card?**
A: You can apply for addition of a member through the state's online portal or local Fair Price Shop/Tehsil office, submitting the birth certificate and other required documents for the new member.

**Q: Can I have two ration cards for the same family?**
A: No, holding multiple ration cards for the same household is not permitted and may be treated as fraud; a family should surrender any additional ration card from a previous residence before getting a new one issued.

**Q: Is a ration card mandatory to receive food subsidies under NFSA?**
A: Yes, a valid ration card (BPL/AAY/Priority Household category) is required to avail subsidized food grains under the National Food Security Act.

**Q: How can I check my ration card application status?**
A: You can check the status online through your respective state's Food and Civil Supplies Department portal using your application reference number or Aadhaar number.
