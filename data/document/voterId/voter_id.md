# Voter ID Card (EPIC - Elector's Photo Identity Card)

## Overview
The Voter ID Card, officially called EPIC (Elector's Photo Identity Card), is an identity document issued by the Election Commission of India (ECI) to eligible citizens for the purpose of voting in elections. It also serves as a valid proof of identity and age for many other purposes.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a citizen of India.
- Must be at least 18 years of age as on the qualifying date (1st January of the year of revision of electoral roll), or as per the applicable cutoff date announced by ECI.
- Must be an ordinary resident of the constituency where registration is sought.
- Should not be disqualified from voting under any law (e.g., unsound mind as declared by a competent court, or disqualified due to corrupt practices or electoral offences).
- A person cannot be registered in more than one constituency at the same time.

## Documents Required
- Proof of age: Birth certificate, Aadhaar card, PAN card, Driving License, Passport, or 10th/12th marksheet.
- Proof of address: Aadhaar card, Passport, Driving License, recent utility bill (electricity, water, gas) in applicant's name, bank/post office passbook, or rent agreement.
- Passport-size photograph (recent, white background preferred).
- Proof of ordinary residence if recently moved (rent agreement, ration card, or utility bill at new address).

## How to Apply
1. **New Registration (Form 6)**: Used by first-time voters or those who have shifted to a new constituency.
2. **Correction in Entries (Form 8)**: Used to correct name, address, date of birth, photo, or other details in an existing entry.
3. **Shifting of Residence (Form 8)**: Used when moving from one constituency to another within the same state or across states.
4. **Deletion/Objection (Form 7)**: Used to object to inclusion of a name or to request deletion (e.g., due to death or shifting).

Applications can be submitted online via the National Voters' Service Portal (NVSP) / Voter Helpline app, or offline at the local Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) office / Booth Level Officer (BLO).

## Processing Time
Typically takes 2 to 4 weeks for verification and issuance, though it can vary by state and workload of the local electoral office. A Booth Level Officer (BLO) usually conducts a field verification visit before approval.

## Common Errors to Avoid
- Mismatch between name spelling on Voter ID application and supporting documents.
- Incorrect or outdated address proof.
- Blurry or non-compliant photograph.
- Applying in a constituency where the applicant is not an ordinary resident.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I vote without a physical Voter ID card?**
A: Yes, if your name is on the electoral roll, you can vote using any of the alternative photo ID documents prescribed by ECI (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, etc.), even without the physical EPIC card.

**Q: How do I check my name on the electoral roll?**
A: You can check via the NVSP website or Voter Helpline app by entering your EPIC number or personal details.

**Q: Is there a fee for applying for a Voter ID?**
A: No, registration and correction services are free of cost.

**Q: Can NRIs register as voters?**
A: Yes, Non-Resident Indians can register using Form 6A, provided they hold a valid Indian passport.

**Q: What if my Voter ID is lost or damaged?**
A: You can apply for a duplicate EPIC card through the local electoral office or NVSP portal by submitting a police complaint copy (for lost cards) and the prescribed fee.
